YOU ARE IN CONTROL

Settings & help

Set up once. Adjust whenever you need.

Connected X account

Open your signed-in X tab, then connect it here. Blocking stops if that account changes.

Automatic X blocking

After a subscription refresh, approved accounts are queued and blocked on X. A dedicated X tab performs the actions.

Hide matching content

Hide posts from approved accounts while you browse. You can reveal individual posts.

Time between blocking actions

Use a steady pace. X errors pause the queue so you can inspect what happened.
